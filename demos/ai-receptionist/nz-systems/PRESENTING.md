# Presenting the NZ Systems demo

NZ Systems is open Monday to Friday, 9 to 5. The demo shows what happens when someone calls
while the shop is closed: Ava answers, takes the details one question at a time, and texts
the team a summary so the job is waiting for the next business day. Every answer has a
one-tap button, so either call clicks through in about a minute.

## Call 1: Saturday morning, fridge not cooling (the priority case)

1. Tap **Call (519) 652-0518**. Ava says the shop's closed and she's taking calls for the team.
2. Tap **My fridge stopped cooling last night and the food is warming up**. Ava picks up the
   appliance and the problem from that one sentence, and says she's flagging it as a
   **PRIORITY** so it's first in line when the shop opens Monday. An amber bar appears.
3. Tap through make and model, name, address and number, then **Yes, that's right**.
4. The team's text: amber **PRIORITY - Fridge/freezer, food spoilage risk** banner,
   **Priority: Priority**, and **Next step: First in line when the shop opens Monday**.

## Call 2: Wednesday evening, washer quit mid-cycle (the routine case)

1. Start over, pick **Washer quit mid-cycle**, then Call.
2. Tap **My washer quit in the middle of a cycle** and the sample answers.
3. The text has no banner: **Priority: Routine**, **Next step: Call back next business day**.

## Worth showing

- **Gas safety:** at any point, type or say *"I smell gas from the stove."* Ava stops the
  intake, tells the caller to leave the house and call 911 or their gas utility, and the text
  arrives with a red **URGENT - Possible gas issue** banner. ("My gas stove won't light" is
  not treated as a gas emergency.)
- **Guardrails:** ask *"How much do you charge?"*, *"When can someone come out?"* or *"Is it
  the compressor?"* Ava won't quote, promise a time, or diagnose, then goes back to her question.
- **"Am I talking to a real person?"** She answers: "I'm an AI receptionist for NZ Systems..."

## Questions they might ask

- **"Does she answer during the day?"** In this demo she covers the closed hours, evenings and
  weekends, which is when calls go unanswered today.
- **"Can we change the questions or what counts as priority?"** Yes, it's all in one settings block.
